THE PIPELINE

Four Acts. One Sequence.

Cheap optical detection gates expensive AI inference — nothing runs unless something's actually there.

Act I · Optical

Illuminate

940nm IR LEDs light the hall in a wavelength the audience can't perceive at all.

Act II · Trigger

Detect the Glint

OpenCV + Hough Circle Transform catch the retro-reflective glint off any lens in the room.

Act III · AI

Confirm

A quantized TFLite model classifies the region — phone, camera, or false alarm.

Act IV · Alert

Raise the Alarm

Confirmed threats push to the dashboard over WebSocket — seat-mapped, in real time.
